🧠 Mathematical Principles & Computational Vectorization

1. Exact GBM Solution (Itô's Lemma)

Under risk-neutral probability \(\mathbb{Q}\), stock price follows:

S(T) = S_0 * exp((r - 0.5*σ²)*T + σ*sqrt(T)*Z)

Where \(Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)\) is generated via the Box-Muller transform.

2. Antithetic Variates & Error Rate

For each shock \(+Z\), we simulate companion \(-Z\). The estimator average:

C_MC = e^(-rT) * (1/N) * Σ 0.5*[f(S_T^(+)) + f(S_T^(-))]

Halves sampling variance, converging strictly at \(\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{N})\).
